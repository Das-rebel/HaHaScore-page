**Honest numbers, not the inflated 0.860.**

Earlier single-fold claims (v7 AUC 0.860, v10 AUC 0.823) were leakage artifacts — speaker-disjoint splits and a 12-video gold set created an identity-channel confound that random K-fold CV hid. The repo's `COUNCIL_FINAL_DECISION.md` documents the audit and `5x3_cv_results.json` records the falsification: a +0.163 per-language normalization claim reversed to −0.127 (p = 0.0004) under 5×3 repeated CV.

What survived the audit:
- Architecture: Cascade Gate (text confidence gates audio) + 1.04M params
- Numbers: 5×3 repeated speaker-disjoint CV, humor AUC 0.6924 ± 0.0359, gold AUC 0.5453 ± 0.1313
- Methodology: a reproducible 5×3 repeated speaker-disjoint CV harness (`laugho_cv.py`) and a falsification experiment (`run_5x3_cv.py`)
- Paper draft: *"Evaluating Speech-Humor Models Without Identity Leakage: A Self-Falsification Case Study"* (in `arxiv_submission/`, submission deferred — see `PIVOT_TO_PAPER.md`)

The transcript was never the joke. The laugh is in the room. The next 0.05 AUC doesn't come from compute. It comes from humans labeling laughter on new clips across more languages — n=12 is too small to support any single-fold number.
