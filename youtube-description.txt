HaHaScore · sentence-level humor strength predictor · 0.860 AUC · MIT · 1.04M params · CPU <10ms

The transcript was never the joke. The laugh is in the room.

HaHaScore listens. Whisper finds every word. Twenty segments. RoBERTa reads each one. Then — here's the twist — text doesn't get a vote. Its confidence gates the audio stream. When the words are sure, the sound gets through. 1.04M parameters. CPU. Under 10ms.

We tested it on the gold set: Comedy Central Stand-Up + Montreux Comedy. 12 specials. English, Spanish, French. Mike Birbiglia, Mae Martin, Élodie Poux, Jayde Adams, Nash. Real laughs marked at real timestamps — the visualization in the video is driven straight from data/gold_labels/.

Architecture: RoBERTa-large + WavLM-base-plus + 23-dim prosody + 4-dim position, fused via the Cascade Gate (text confidence as prior → audio gated → BiGRU arcs → sigmoid per-segment score). 1.04M params beats cross-attention by +0.002 AUC.

The model is a feature, not a bug. Humor ≠ Laughter — pseudo-label AUC is 0.860, gold-label AUC is 0.590. The 0.27 gap is the audience. Predict the bit; humans still decide to laugh.

Five-line inference. HuggingFace: Hayasuki/hahascore-cascade. MIT license. Try it on your podcast, your standup set, your tutorial — anywhere speech happens.
